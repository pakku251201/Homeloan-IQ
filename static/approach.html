ACT 1 · DATA FOUNDATION

Bring the evidence together

Home-loan assessment depends on borrower details, income and account signals, repayment history, bureau information, property checks and documents. LoanPilot loads synthetic structured records into BigQuery and joins them into one applicant view.

01 · Ingest

CSV source records with stable IDs.

02 · Join

BigQuery tables and a gold applicant view.

03 · Inspect

Missing values, flags and evidence at case level.

ACT 2 · AGENTIC WORKFLOW

Make each check explainable

The prototype applies explicit rules to credit score, repayment burden (FOIR), loan-to-value (LTV), KYC, documents, repayment bounces and property-charge flags. Each flag includes a reason and a policy label.

Intake

Check key fields and unusual values.

Assessment

Run affordability, credit and collateral checks.

Human review

Officer resolves exceptions and records the final choice.

What this demo proves

A shared evidence view and explicit decision rules can make a loan case easier to inspect and audit. The current engine is deterministic, not an LLM-powered agent; bureau and CERSAI data are mocked. It does not sanction real loans.
