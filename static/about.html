Purpose

LoanPilot illustrates how structured lending data and explicit policy checks can support an officer through early home-loan assessment. It is designed for a presales discussion, not for real lending.

What is included

  • 600 generated applicants in the source-data generator.
  • Mock bureau and property-charge checks.
  • BigQuery joined applicant view and officer-decision log.
  • Case-level reasons and a manual officer decision control.
Responsible use

All records are synthetic. Recommendations are demonstration outputs, not credit advice, actual eligibility determinations or regulatory interpretations.
